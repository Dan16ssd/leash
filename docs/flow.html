Leash, drawn as a network

Think of an AI agent as a host that can send money. Leash is the firewall in front of it. Ordinary firewalls check the destination and the size. Leash also checks which zone the decision came from.

The one rule: if the recipient, amount or price came from the untrusted zone, drop the transaction. It doesn't matter how small the amount is or how convincing the prompt was. Everything else (limits, allowlists, RWA holders) is a normal ACL on top.
